# Neral I1 targeted training data

The I1 campaign supplements the pinned human OpenAssistant/oasst1 instruction corpus with deterministic project-generated examples for measured weak areas: German answer quality, mathematics, Python/code and short reasoning.

These examples are **not generated by ChatGPT, OpenAI APIs, Qwen or another language model**. `build_targeted_sft50_sources.py` creates prompts and answers from fixed templates, seeded pseudorandom numbers and directly computed results. Mathematics answers are calculated in code; Python examples are template-derived; German and reasoning examples use finite project-owned templates.

The generated sources are written locally, SHA-256 pinned in the SFT source manifest, then passed through the same cleaning, split, benchmark-contamination and exact-deduplication pipeline as the human data. Normalized user prompts are assigned to a split before train quotas are selected, so identical normalized prompts cannot cross train/validation/test/holdout.

The supplemental `benchmarks50/targeted_v1.jsonl` suite is explicitly screened from this generated SFT data. Because Targeted v1 was frozen after B3 pretraining had already begun, it is treated as a **supplemental diagnostic**, not retroactively claimed as a fully contamination-proof release benchmark and not folded into NIS 1.0.

Current intended SFT mixture for I1 D3:

- human OASST1 English: 27%
- human OASST1 German: 3%
- generated German core: 20%
- generated mathematics: 24% total, split DE/EN
- generated Python/code: 20% total, split DE/EN
- generated short reasoning: 6% total, split DE/EN

The final prepared dataset remains local and is not committed. Only source code, provenance metadata, audits and benchmark reports belong in GitHub.
