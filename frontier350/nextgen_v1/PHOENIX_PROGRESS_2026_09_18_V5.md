## Known constraints — do not represent these as complete

- **Actual 350M pilot remains NOT executed**. A self-hosted CPU test of a tiny synthetic network neither proves 350M GPU forward/backward viability/VRAM margin, nor throughput, loss gain, end-to-end 350M recovery or improved free answers.
- The manifest SHA and checkpoint lineage bind `stop_after_new_tokens` exactly. Resuming after a crash *towards the same approved stop* is designed and the toy structural checkpoint replay passes; deliberately extending from an earlier staged stop to a later one is **not implemented**. Never evade this with an edited manifest or mismatched lineage.
- Generic native *fresh* trainers still permit a non-aligned early stop, which a later exact resume rejects; new weight-only pilot rejects it prelaunch. Full RNG-payload validation and full-size AdamW recovery remain open. Data SHA preflight does not freeze files against mutation afterward, or check semantic overlap. Operators must verify all real training data and holdout independently.
- Existing GitHub-hosted PR CI has reported failures with absent steps or unavailable blob logs; no hosted-green claim. Only linked self-hosted CPU runs are substantiated. Synthetic manifests are fictional test fixtures, not authorizations.
- No comparison can be called NIS2-v2 or a quality improvement until a genuinely independent frozen bilingual rubric-based holdout is complete and blind identical free-generation base/candidate results measured. A short 262k pilot is a controlled experiment, not a guarantee of substantial quality improvement, and throughput of 60–70k accepted *training* tokens/s has not been established.
