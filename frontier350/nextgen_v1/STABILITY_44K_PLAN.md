# NextGen 44k stability qualification — research-only protocol

Decision: prioritize **>=44,000 quality-accepted, END-TO-END training tokens/s**, not 50k peak synthetic GPU compute. This revision does **not** establish a new measured throughput. Current best historical synthetic upper-four-block run is 44,839 tok/s; its artificial repeated batch, random model weights and exclusion of most transformer backward passes make it *incomparable* to real training.

## Order of work / stop rules

1. **Protect current assets.** No restart of failed V8.1, no merge to production, no overwrite of checkpoint or token ledger. Audit the latest healthy own-weight checkpoint and its model/tokenizer/optimizer/RNG/data-order SHA identities. Verify exact-resume by a stop/restart experiment on a copy, compare resulting weights, AdamW state, accepted-token ledger, RNG and data sequence against uninterrupted control. Any mismatch stops this qualification.
2. **Instrument the actual bottleneck.** With trusted, licensed, filtered *local* real training data and an isolated checkpoint, account for corpus I/O, CPU/H2D wait, forward/backward, optimizer, synchronizations, validation, checkpoint write/verification, recoveries, rejected attempts and total monotonic wall time. No short synthetic probe results in the throughput numerator. Record temperatures, reserved/free VRAM, compiler warmup and abort causes. Do not relax <=82 C or <=9 GiB experimental limits, or project SAFE limits.
3. **A/B for model quality with fixed conditions.** Keep own Neral architecture/weights, tokenizer, tokens/data order, random seeds, optimizer objective/LR and evaluation protocol comparable. Collect frozen baseline AND candidate held-out validation loss, repetition, free generation/EOS correctness, German/English, math, code, science and full NIS2. Independently audit source licenses, PII, duplicate/holdout overlap and exact case inventory; the previous 140-case probe is not full NIS2. A mere lower synthetic loss on repeated tokens is not quality improvement.
4. **Stability gate.** On three consecutive complete stages of >=5,000,000 *accepted* tokens each, measure accepted tokens divided by total stage wall time including I/O, evaluation, checkpoint/recovery. EACH stage and pooled three-stage rate must be >=44,000 tok/s, zero rejected tokens, healthy finite loss/gradients, no guards tripped or quality regression. No single fast stage or pooled average can hide a slow stage. Store logs, input/checkpoint hashes, GPU telemetry and independent summaries as immutable artifacts.
5. **Only after evidence:** run an independent exact-resume repeat and sealed untouched holdout, verify full NIS2 and per-category non-regression; then review isolated draft PR. Do not automatically merge, promote or start a long production run. If real 44k proves infeasible on RTX4070, report the measured sustainable accepted rate instead of relabeling synthetic tok/s.

## Tooling boundary

`admission.py` checks claimed JSON against a 44k threshold and several quality/safety flags but does **not** authenticate raw artifacts, verify that stage windows are consecutive, independently run full NIS2 or execute a trainer. Its `admitted` field means only that self-reported *contract fields* passed; it is **not release approval**. `probe.py`/`compare_probes.py` preserve historical 50k *synthetic diagnostic* fields for older runs; never substitute them as evidence for this 44k acceptance threshold. `train_sft_pilot.py` remains opt-in and unexecuted. A real exactly-resumable NextGen trainer must be developed and validated before claiming the gate is met.
