## Required local provenance receipt for every approved source

Record `source_id`, original Hub repository and URL, exact immutable Hub commit, actual file SHA-256 and selected row IDs, precise dataset/subset license and attribution, human license/privacy review evidence, transformed-record provenance, and timestamp. Source-specific human checks must be completed **before** setting any `approved_for_training`, `privacy_reviewed`, `benchmark_screened` fields in `sft_contract.py` manifests; these booleans are operator attestations and are not verified legal facts.

1. Prepare only an explicitly reviewed, small DE/EN subset on the user's PC. Keep source text, human review records and frozen NIS2 holdout offline and private. Never upload raw corpora, prompts, answers, checkpoints or manifest paths to GitHub, Adobe or Hugging Face by default.
2. Deduplicate by normalized prompts and conversation lineage **before** splitting. Split at conversation/source-group level, not individual message level. Treat benchmark overlap, PII and role contamination as stop conditions, not post-training warnings.
3. Run `phoenix_data_audit.py` locally. Its `approved:false` report is diagnostic and must never be reinterpreted as the independent screening report accepted by `sft_contract.py`.
4. After documented independent review, create the training pack locally and perform a bounded SFT **only if** the RTX 4070 is actually idle, checkpoint copy is SHA-pinned, and all existing training safety gates pass. Keep the production weights unchanged.
5. Measure true raw baseline/candidate answers on identical frozen prompts. Require independent correctness and the full NIS2 protocol before considering an experiment successful. A loss decrease, permissive license tag, or synthetic CI pass is never evidence of model quality.
